# Who am I? 🧐
Hello! 👋 I'm Izabela, and I like making sure systems show exactly what they should.

My path into tech started with assembling my first computer and repairing laptops, smartphones and other devices in service centres. There I also worked as a QA, checking phone PCBs after refurbishment.

I studied Computer Science for 5 semesters with an E-commerce Developer specialisation (PHP, Magento): products, variants, prices and availability in an online store. Then I completed two manual testing courses and built a QA project.

Since April 2026 I've worked in a Burger King restaurant (Rex Concepts), and since September 2026 I've been running shifts as a junior shift manager. I see every day what happens when a product, price or modifier in the system doesn't match what the kitchen and the guest expect.

Today I combine all of this: **accuracy with data and system configuration, testing changes, and automating repetitive processes.**

# What I work on now 🎯
+ **Product and menu data** - checking that products, prices, modifiers and availability are consistent across sales channels
+ **Testing changes** - verifying that a change works as intended and describing bugs so they can be fixed quickly
+ **Process automation** - building workflows in n8n and Make.com that replace manual, repetitive work
+ **Process documentation** - turning procedures into clear, easy-to-use tools for the team
